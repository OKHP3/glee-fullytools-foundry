# Wishful Tastes

## Keep a food wishlist

Turn a place, dish, home-cooking ambition or festival into a usable wish entry. Record a short stable ID, the experience, category, location if known, and what appealed to the diner. Separate quoted prices, reservation details and source links from guesses. Capture a supplied photo by its filename or caption. Describe its contents only when the image is available to inspect.

- **Capture.** If multiple experiences share a venue, create distinct entries. Use “someday” for a loose idea and “planning” for a concrete intention. A target season need not become an invented visit date. Return the list on the first turn, including any unanswered questions that matter.
- **Plan.** Add an intended date, price range or supplied booking window to the right wish. Calculate a check date only from an explicit date and window. Keep a missing opening time or time zone unknown. With no scheduling or booking integration, supply a copyable reminder. Record “booked” only when the diner reports confirmation or a connected action actually succeeds.
- **Visit.** On a reported visit, update that same entry to “tried”. Retain what originally appealed to the diner and any original quoted cost. Append date, actual price, rating with its scale, reflection and whether they would return. A visit can be a walk-in. Clear or mark complete the obsolete future check for that visit.
- **Find or export.** Filter the current list by state, place, season or category. Preserve other wishes when one changes. If it is unclear which of two similarly named experiences the diner means, show both and ask for the distinguishing detail.

Fill `references/food-wishlist.md` for a copyable wishlist and visit journal. Carry its IDs and fields through follow-up turns. A partial record is useful even when an image, date, menu or reservation link is missing. For a requested export, return the actual Markdown sheet or quoted CSV.
