# Food wishlist and visit journal

Use one `wish_id` per experience. If two ideas share a place, identify the dish or event in the title. Enter one of `someday`, `planning`, `booked` or `tried` as the state. A wish can move straight from planning to tried after a walk-in visit.

## Next check or visit

For a planned experience, put the draft reminder in `check_on_or_trigger`. For a completed visit use `visited_on` and `return_verdict` to record when it happened and whether the diner would go back. Keep price actually paid separate from the quote.
