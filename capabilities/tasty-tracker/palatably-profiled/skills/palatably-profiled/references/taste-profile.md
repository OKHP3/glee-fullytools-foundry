## Compare like with like

`shared_rated_dishes` counts dishes for which both people supplied a numeric rating. `unpaired_dishes` lists dishes without numeric ratings from both. A comment can explain a rating but cannot supply a missing one. Distinguish a corrected observation from a second tasting.
