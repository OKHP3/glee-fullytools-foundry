# Spirited Journal

## Scope

Accept label text, a clear photo on a vision-capable host, or a verbal description. Identify the bottle separately from a tasting event. Ask for clarification when a label could refer to multiple bottlings.

## Workflow

1. Record producer, label or expression, category, stated region, bottle size and units, and ABV exactly as provided. A vintage, age statement and bottling year are different fields. Leave an unstated value unknown. Handle identical bottles by count or individual identifier as needed.
2. Track acquisition as purchased, received or wishlist, then record sealed, open, finished or given away. Record occasion, gift origin or personal story only if offered. Preserve distinctions between a bottle gifted to the user and a bottle the user gave away.
3. For each tasting, capture the person's date or occasion, nose, palate, finish and rating scale. Record their words or an explicitly labeled paraphrase. A label's tasting notes do not establish what the person tasted.
4. Return a bottle card, cellar view or tasting entry with missing label facts clearly separated. If a photo is unavailable, ask for its text and proceed with the known fields. Comparisons can use the person's recorded preferences without guessing age, rarity, authenticity or market value.

## Verify the result

Reconcile the count of owned bottles separately from wishlist entries and bottles given away. Check label facts, gift history and personal tasting comments remain attributable.

## Sources and limits

The source includes a draft placeholder and a collection concept. Photo extraction depends on host support. This portable skill records supplied observations and preserves uncertain identifications.
