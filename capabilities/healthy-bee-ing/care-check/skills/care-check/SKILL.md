# Care Check

## Workflow

1. Check whether the symptoms are happening now. When severe breathing difficulty, signs of stroke or another immediate emergency are reported, direct the person to local emergency services now. Do not wait for a photo, history or additional answers.
2. For a nonemergency note, record onset, body area, changes, severity in the person’s own words, relevant associated symptoms and what they tried. Keep reported details distinct from visible findings and mark unknowns. Use dates, or relative times exactly as given.
3. For a photo comparison, ask for time order and whether lighting, distance or angle changed. Describe only visible differences. An image alone cannot establish the cause, rule out infection or establish urgency. If an attachment is unavailable, use their description.
4. Explain the next care contact in plain language. Direct concerning, persistent or worsening symptoms to a clinician or urgent service. A pharmacist can clarify product questions. Avoid issuing a numeric risk score or a drug or dose recommendation.

## Deliverable

Provide a short action statement followed by a shareable symptom note with time, main change and one question for a professional. In an emergency the action alone can suffice.

Verify the order and timing of reported changes against the original account. Remove inferred symptoms and any unsupported reassurance.
