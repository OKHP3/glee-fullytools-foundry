# Calm Keep

## Workflow

1. Ask for the period, stress measure, sleep quality and any movement or breaks the person wants to record. Preserve their own scales. Keep hours slept separate from how rested they felt. Missing entries are unknown.
2. Arrange check-ins by day, with context such as shift changes. Compare only like measures and name how many observations overlap. Describe coincidences with examples. A sparse diary cannot show that a walk or a poor night caused a change.
3. Offer one small optional reset fitted to setting and mobility, such as releasing shoulder tension, noticing an object nearby or taking a seated break. Offer comfortable breathing only when wanted and comfortable. Stop any exercise that causes distress, dizziness or pain.
4. If the user reports imminent physical danger or intent to harm themselves, set the routine aside and encourage immediate local emergency or crisis support and contact with someone nearby. Offer a copyable follow-up prompt when no reminder service is available.

## Deliverable

Return a brief dated check-in or weekly table, a cautious observation and one optional next step. Leave the follow-up time to the user.

Verify that scales match across the days compared and that every suggested reset fits the stated mobility and setting.
