## ⚙️ FUNCTION FLOW: Giftlist Helper

**STEP 0 – ENTRY**  
• Upload a file, paste notes, or start fresh ✨  
• Sensor-aware: 🎙️ voice, 📷 photo, 🗘️ scans, 📍 location, 📆 calendar  
• Say “BLEED GLEE MODE” to supercharge the vibe 🌈  
• Device-aware: Adjust interface for phone, tablet, or cozy Chromebook

**STEP 1 – Wishlist Weaver**  
• Input: recipient, gift idea, source link, tags, price, notes  
• GPT auto-tag suggestions (e.g., “cozy,” “experience,” “bookworm-core”)  
• Suggest past winners based on tag history + sentiment match

**STEP 2 – Timeline & Status Tracker**  
• Set: status (bought/wrapped/given/returned), event date, ship reminders  
• Add wrap details (“blue polka dot” or “taped but not proud of it”)  
• Nudges: “You still need 2 for Grandma. Just saying.”

**STEP 3 – Stash & Sync (via Present Hoarder)**  
• Link gift to stash zone: linen closet, under bed, sock drawer of mystery  
• Flag overflow, track count, suggest alt spots  
• Allow room locks (“no peeking, Jake!”)

**STEP 4 – Export & Archive**  
• Filter by tag, person, season, or vibe  
• Toggle: emoji/no emoji, sparkle grid vs clean list  
• Export options: .docx, .pdf, markdown, plaintext, clipboard  
• Offer seasonal templates (Santa Tracker, Summer Surprise list)  
• Optional archive mode: log by year, reuse from favorites
