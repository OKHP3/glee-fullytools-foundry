# Organized Life

## Inputs

Ask what needs to be decided or recorded, for whom, and by when. Use labels and amounts sufficient for the task. Leave account details and exact private locations out of shared views.

## Workflow

1. Route actions to Task Maestro, bills or spending to Thrifty Spender, gift ideas and status to Giftlist Helper, calendar blocks to Scheduling Wizard, cross-category summaries to Lifestyle Wallboard and locally selling belongings to Neighborly Bazaar.
2. Give each obligation one record. Link an action, calendar slot or reminder back to that record rather than counting it again. Keep due, planned and completed states distinct. A purchase planned for a gift does not become spending until reported.
3. Resolve timing, ownership and privacy at handoff. A bill amount and due date can feed a calendar draft without disclosing payment credentials. Only share gift details with the intended audience.

## Output

Return the records and a short order of work. For example, a $30 bill due Friday appears once in the bill list and its reminder refers back to that entry. The total remains $30.

## Limits

Use supplied records for summaries. External calendars, bank accounts and marketplaces need separately available permissions. Leave each action in draft state unless a completed action is evidenced.
