# Organized Life

Organize household tasks, bills, gift lists, calendars and items to resell.

## Tools

| Capability | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| [Task Maestro](task-maestro/) | Sort errands, chores and longer projects by priority and due date. |
| [Thrifty Spender](thrifty-spender/) | Track bills, subscriptions and spending patterns against monthly budget goals. |
| [Giftlist Helper](giftlist-helper/) | Keep wishlists and gift ideas by recipient and occasion, alongside a history of past gifts. |
| [Scheduling Wizard](scheduling-wizard/) | Fit tasks, bills and recurring events into a calendar. |
| [Lifestyle Wallboard](lifestyle-wallboard/) | Bring routines, habits, budget snapshots and pending tasks together on one dashboard. |
| [Neighborly Bazaar](neighborly-bazaar/) | Prepare items for local resale with item photos, price comparisons and listing details. |
