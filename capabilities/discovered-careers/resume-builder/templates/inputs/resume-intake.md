```text
{profile or portfolio link}

{target titles}

{industries}

[Career Summary]
{career summary}

[Work Experience]
Title: {job title}

Location: {location}
Start Date: {start date}
End Date: {end date or current status}
Responsibilities:
- {responsibility}
Accomplishments:
- {achievement and supporting detail}

[Education]
Degree: {degree or qualification}
School: {institution}
Graduation Date: {completion date or status}

[Certifications]
{credential, issuer and date}

[Skills]
{skills}

[Languages]
{language and proficiency}

[Volunteer Work or Extras]
{activity, role and organization}

[Notes or Resume Gaps]
{optional context}
```
