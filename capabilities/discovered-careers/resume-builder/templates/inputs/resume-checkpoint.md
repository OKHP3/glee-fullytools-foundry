# Resume checkpoint

Blank public template. Save a compact record of the details gathered so far. Repeat an entry for each role or qualification.

```text
[Name]
{full name}

[Preferred Job Titles]
{target titles}

[Contact Information]
Email: {email address}
Phone: {phone number}
LinkedIn: {profile link}

[Career Summary]
{career summary}

[Work Experience]
- {job title} | {employer} | {location} | {date range}
  - {responsibility}
  - {achievement and supporting detail}

[Education]
{qualification}, {institution}, {completion date or status}

[Skills]
{skills}

[Certifications]
{credentials}

[Languages]
{language and proficiency}

[Volunteer Work]
{role}, {organization}, {date range}

[Career Gaps or Notes]
{optional context}
```
