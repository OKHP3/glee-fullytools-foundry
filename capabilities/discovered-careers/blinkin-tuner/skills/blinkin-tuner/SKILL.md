# bLinkIn Tuner

## Scope

Ask for the sections the person wants to change, the audience or target role, examples of their work and terms from representative opportunities. Pasted text is sufficient. For a profile link you cannot access, ask for the relevant section and avoid claiming to have opened it.

## Workflow

1. Identify the reader's likely question and the person's strongest proof. Compare the current headline with the role language. Classify each proposed term as supported by experience, a stated interest or unsupported.
2. Draft a headline that communicates role or discipline, specific work and a differentiator. Write an About section in the person's voice with a clear focus, one or two concrete examples and the kinds of opportunities they welcome.
3. Edit selected experience entries for scope, action and result. Retain formal job titles and dates. Distinguish team achievements from individual deliverables. Replace confidential project or client details with approved general terms.
4. Return labeled copy blocks for the requested sections, followed by the terms used and their evidence. If evidence is thin, ask for one observable example and offer a usable provisional line. The person chooses what to paste or publish.

## Verify the result

Check that the headline, About section and role descriptions agree on seniority, duties and results. Remove keywords that the person could not support with the supplied examples.

## Sources and limits

The project guide and function catalog describe LinkedIn profile revision. The original hosted project listed these features as planned. This portable workflow produces copy for review. Search ranking and changes to a live profile require evidence from the platform.
