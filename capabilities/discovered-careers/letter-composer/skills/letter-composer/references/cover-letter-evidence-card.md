# Cover letter evidence card

Use one card for a claim that will appear in the letter. The four fields keep the letter specific when the applicant contributed to a group result.

| Field | What to record |
| --- | --- |
| Need | One duty or problem named in the posting. |
| My action | The applicant's own verb, the object of their work, and their scope. |
| Outcome | The observed result, who achieved it and any supplied measure. Write “unknown” if needed. |
| Connection | How that example could help with the advertised duty. |

## Convert a card into a paragraph

Open with the example, connect it to the advertised duty, then cut the explanation if the relationship is already clear. Keep the card outside the copy-ready letter.

For example, the posting asks for reliable shift handovers. The applicant wrote a checklist for a team pilot. The team used it across three shifts. “I wrote the checklist our team piloted across three shifts. I would bring the same care to handovers in this role” preserves individual and team credit. It makes no claim about reducing errors without evidence.

## Revision checklist

1. Update every place the corrected claim appears, including any summary and evidence card.
2. Apply the requested tone, audience and length while preserving the facts.
3. Use the named contact if supplied. Otherwise use a neutral greeting such as “Dear Hiring Team”.
4. Keep drafting notes and unresolved facts outside the copyable text. Add a signature only when a name or signature was supplied.
