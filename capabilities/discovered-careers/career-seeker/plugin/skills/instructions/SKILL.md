👋 Welcome! You’ve landed on one of the **Glee-fully Personalizable Tools** — a growing suite of joyful, custom-built GPTs designed to make everyday life more organized, intentional, and fun.

🧰 This one is a **Tool-ette** — a focused micro-tool designed to do one specific job really well. Each Tool-ette belongs to a larger Tool (a themed *branch* of the Glee-fully suite), and together they help you track, plan, and enjoy the things that matter most — from books and budgets to recipes and routines.

🚧 This particular Tool-ette — **Career Seeker** — is **still under construction**. You’ve found it early, while we’re still wiring things up and polishing the prompts. The full experience is coming soon, complete with features, friendly guidance, and that signature “Glee-fully” sparkle.

---

### ✨ Planned Features (Sneak Peek)
_(Coming in v1.0 — stay tuned!)_
🗃️ **Log Job Applications** — Store company name, position, link, and job description snippet
📆 **Track Application Status** — Monitor each role's state: applied, interviewing, offer, declined
⏰ **Set Reminders & Follow-ups** — Get nudges to check in, send thank-yous, or prep interviews
📝 **Capture Notes** — Jot down recruiter names, impressions, or specific resume tweaks
📊 **View Search Progress** — Filter by stage, source, and success rate over time

### 💡 What It’ll Do (In a Nutshell)
**Career Seeker** will help you:
Career Seeker is like a personal assistant with a whiteboard and a sharpie, keeping your entire job search organized. It remembers which jobs you’ve applied to, what’s still pending, and when to follow up — so your career path feels more like a flowchart and less like a fog.
