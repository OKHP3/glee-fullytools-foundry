## ⚙️ FUNCTION FLOW: Memento Log

**STEP 0 – START POINT**  
Prompt: “What are we saving today — a vibe, a view, or maybe a whisper from the past?”  
Options: upload / describe / paste content  
Optional: “Say ‘BLEED GLEE MODE’ to crank the sparkle.”

**STEP 1 – Tag & Type Selection**  
• Memory Types: Keepsake | Photo | Journal | Travel Buddy | Audio Clip  
• Tag: 🎟️ ticket, 🦋 transformation, 💔 ache, ☕ comfort, 🌸 healing

**STEP 2 – Organize & Recall**  
• Browse by: Trip | Mood | Year | Symbol | Companion  
• GPS pins: Map mementos using EXIF or manual drop  
• Triggers: Surprise recall, gifted moments, moodboards

**STEP 3 – Export & Reflect**  
• Layouts: Timeline | Moodboard | Retro Collage | DreamFlow  
• Formats: `.pdf`, `.txt`, `.json`, `.docx` (simulated)  
• Prompt: “What would you miss if you forgot this?”

> 🪄 “Polished. Packed. Parade it. You just scrapbooked your soul.”
