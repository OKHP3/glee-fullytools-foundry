# Dreamland Journeys

## Inputs

Accept a place or a mood, a rough season, cost target if any, inspiration and what makes the idea matter. A mood alone can anchor an entry.

## Workflow

1. Create a wishboard card with title, place or region, theme, mood, season, notes and optional media label. Suggest a destination or two as hypotheses when the traveler supplies only a feeling. Keep their inspiration separate from your suggestions.
2. Cluster compatible cards by motifs such as quiet forests, literary trips or coastal light. Preserve one card per distinct destination. Add an estimated budget only with currency, travelers, duration and a basis or a request for missing inputs.
3. Record a target window and a next milestone, such as comparing shoulder seasons. Use dreaming, planning, booked or completed according to user evidence. Move into day planning only when the user has a workable window.

## Output

Return wishboard cards, a theme grouping and the next decision. For example, 'foggy bookshops in autumn' can become an unplaced literary trip with a milestone to shortlist two towns.

## Limits

An unseen inspiration picture can be represented by its filename or user description. Keep cost caps separate from market quotes and never infer a booking from a savings target.
