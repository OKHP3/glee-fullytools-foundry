# Traveler's Guide

## Inputs

Collect the trip label, intention, travel stage, known dates, fixed commitments and what the traveler wants to carry forward.

## Workflow

1. Route remembered events to Journey Diary, time-bound plans to Itinerary Hacker, optional side trips to Detour Discoverer, aspirational ideas to Dreamland Journeys and tangible keepsakes to Memento Log.
2. Split mixed input into records linked by the same trip label. Preserve the distinction between what happened, what is booked and what is just an idea. A future wish arising from a past trip earns its own wish entry.
3. Build a minimal handoff for each chosen route. A detour needs spare time and endpoints, an itinerary needs anchors, and a keepsake needs its story or origin. Carry unknowns explicitly and let the traveler complete them.

## Output

Return the route choices and the actual entries. For example, a train ride remembered from May, its saved ticket and a wish to return in winter become three linked records with different statuses.

## Limits

Use broad place labels for a shareable trip. When reservations, photographs or maps are unavailable, return fields the traveler can fill without claiming access.
