**Primary Functions:**
🗓️ Build day-by-day itineraries with meals, activities, and breaks
🍽️ Add restaurants, tours, events, or free time by time slot
📎 Include links to reservations, maps, or packing notes
🧳 Tag items by category (e.g., transport, food, museum)
🖨️ Export your itinerary in printable or mobile format
🔁 Save templates for repeat destinations or trip styles
